Our Story

Rooted in Sinai

Sharm Excursions was founded in 2010 by Ahmed and his brother Khaled, two Sharm El Sheikh locals who grew up swimming these reefs and riding these deserts. What started as a small boat tour operation has grown into Sharm's most trusted full-service excursion provider.

Our philosophy has never changed: treat every guest like family. That means honest pricing (no hidden fees — ever), genuine hotel pickup, and guides who actually care about giving you the best day of your holiday.

We're not a call centre or an online aggregator. When you WhatsApp us, you're talking to someone who lives here — someone who snorkeled Ras Mohammed last Tuesday and rode a quad through the desert last weekend.

What We Stand For

Our Values

🚫
Zero Hidden Fees
The price you see on our website is the price you pay. We quote in US Dollars and there are no taxes, booking fees, or surprise charges added at checkout or on the day.
💳
Pay on Arrival
We never take your money in advance. Reserve your spot for free, pay cash directly to our guide on the day of your tour. If something comes up, there's nothing to chase back.
🏨
Free Hotel Pickup
Every single excursion includes door-to-door hotel pickup and drop-off. We come to you — you never have to figure out how to get somewhere on your own.
🌿
Respect for Nature
We operate under strict environmental guidelines. Reef-safe sunscreen is mandatory on all water tours. We never allow guests to touch or collect coral, and we work closely with Ras Mohammed National Park.
🤝
Community First
We work exclusively with local Bedouin guides, local boat captains, and local drivers. Every booking you make puts money directly into Sharm El Sheikh's community.
